under the Creative Commons licence its photographer chose; the attribution
below is also shown on the card at run time. Non-commercial licences
(CC BY-NC, CC BY-NC-SA) permit this non-commercial distribution; anyone
redistributing LARMOR commercially must remove those images. The CC BY-NC-ND
image and the "all rights reserved" image that earlier builds carried were
